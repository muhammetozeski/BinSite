How the ecosystem works

Your card is applied first, then the weather event, then the seasonal update.

Each season consumes 7 water and 8 food. Each 15 pollution adds 1 water loss; each 20 pollution adds 1 forest loss.

Forest gains 3 when water is above 20; otherwise it loses 5. Wildlife gains 4 when forest is at least 40 and water at least 25; otherwise it loses 7.

If water, forest, wildlife, or food reaches zero, the run ends. After twelve seasons, a sustainable balance requires water ≥20, forest ≥35, wildlife ≥35, food ≥15, and pollution ≤45.
