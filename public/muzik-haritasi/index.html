Music Map

Place sound nodes in space. Move through them to turn your route into a composition.

In edit mode, click empty space to add a node or drag one to move it. In walk mode, hold the pointer and approach the notes.

Horizontal position sets stereo balance. A node triggers at most once per second. Record up to 24 nodes, 200 notes and a one-minute route. WAV export synthesizes actual stereo samples from your visits.
