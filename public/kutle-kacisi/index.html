Your mission

Approach the cyan capsules within 28 units while keeping your speed below 90 to collect them. Once all three are rescued, enter the green exit ring on the left. Hitting the planet or leaving the map ends the flight.

Fuel is used only while thrusting. Gravity grows stronger near the planet. To slow down, turn against your direction of travel and apply thrust.

Flight controls

A / D or Left / Right rotate the ship. Space applies thrust. Hold the on-screen buttons to play on a phone.

A standalone local physics game. Shared links reproduce the capsule layout; other player results are not collected.
