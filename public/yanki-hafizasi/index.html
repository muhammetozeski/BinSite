How to play

Wait while the sequence plays. When the display says “Your turn”, repeat the same buttons in order. Each successful round adds one step.

Use the mouse, touch controls, or the 1, 2, 3, 4 keys. Each sound is also represented by a color, shape, and number.

Try rhythm mode

In rhythm mode, tap briefly for a short note and hold a long note for at least 450 milliseconds before releasing. Long notes are labeled “long” during playback.

Mute mode preserves every visual cue. Hold duration does not matter in order-only mode. Complete twenty rounds to finish. Records stay in this browser.
