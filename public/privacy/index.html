Privacy and data

See what is saved, who can read it and how to remove it.

Standalone tools

Games may store local scores, and editors may store drafts, favorites, and preferences in your browser. Clearing browser data can erase these records. Download important projects when an export option is available.

Share links

Some editors include the created content inside a share link. Anyone receiving that link can read and forward it. Do not include passwords or private information.

Shared communities

Public contributions may be visible to other visitors. Contribution limits and reports use an app identity; it does not verify who a person is.

Private exchange messages and room clues are returned only to the relevant browser identities. Read the rules shown inside the product before submitting content.

Time capsules

Capsule links are not publicly listed. The message stays locked until its opening time, but it is not an end-to-end encrypted vault. Anyone with the link can see its title and opening time, then read the message when it opens.

Accounts and the project vault

Your account uses a session cookie. Anyone with your recovery key can access the account; store it securely. Files in your project vault belong to your account and can be deleted from the account page. Existing files are not automatically deleted when a paid allowance ends.

Payments

When billing is enabled, card details are entered on the payment provider's page. BinSite does not store card numbers. Payment references may be saved to check your membership. Returning from checkout does not grant paid access without provider verification.

Advertising

When enabled, advertisements are labelled. The ad provider applies its own data policy and configured consent handling. Clicking an ad is never required to use a product.

Abuse controls

Hashed app and connection identifiers help limit spam and distinguish reports. Cloudflare may process infrastructure logs while providing the service. Report inappropriate public content using the product's report control.
