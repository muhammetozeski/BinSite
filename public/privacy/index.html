Privacy and data

This page explains where product data is stored and how sharing works.

Standalone tools

Games may store local scores, and editors may store drafts, favorites, and preferences in your browser. Clearing browser data can erase these records. Download important projects when an export option is available.

Share links

Some editors include the created content inside a share link. Anyone receiving that link can read and forward it. Do not include passwords or private information.

Shared communities

Public stories, polls, canvases, debates, and other community contributions are stored in the shared database and may be visible to other visitors. A random browser identity supports contribution limits and abuse reporting. It does not verify a real person's identity.

Private exchange messages and room clues are returned only to the relevant browser identities. Read the rules shown inside the product before submitting content.

Time capsules

Capsule links are not listed publicly. The server withholds the message before its opening time. This is not an end-to-end encrypted vault: the message is stored on the server. People with the link can see the title and opening time, then read the message once it opens.

Accounts and the project vault

A server-readable session cookie protects your account. Anyone with your recovery key can access the account; store it securely. Files in your project vault belong to your account and can be deleted from the account page. Existing files are not automatically deleted when a paid allowance ends.

Payments

When billing is enabled, card details are entered on the payment provider's page. BinSite does not store card numbers. The server may store provider transaction, customer, and subscription identifiers to verify access. Returning from checkout does not grant paid access without provider verification.

Advertising

When enabled, advertisements are labelled. The ad provider applies its own data policy and configured consent handling. Clicking an ad is never required to use a product.

Abuse controls

The server may use digests of browser identities and connection addresses to limit requests and distinguish reports. Cloudflare may process infrastructure logs while providing the service. Report inappropriate public content using the product's report control.
