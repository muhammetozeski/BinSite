How the twins move

Up and Down move both characters the same way. Right moves blue right and pink left; Left does the opposite. A blocked character stays still while the other may move.

Both characters must occupy their own green exit on the same turn . Reaching an exit early does not freeze that character. Generated maze pairs are checked for a solution in their combined state space.

Use the arrow keys or on-screen controls. Hints consider both current positions. Score: 2000 − moves × 25 − hints × 150. This local game reproduces the same walls from a shared link.
