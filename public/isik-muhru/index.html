Mirror workshop

Click a mirror tile to switch its direction between / claimed \ orientations. Mirrors turn rays by 90 degrees. Dark blocks stop light.

The target circle must receive all required colors . Red + blue makes purple; red + green makes yellow; all three make white.

Rays continue after crossing the target. A misdirected ray leaves the board or stops when it repeats a loop.

Each layout has verified solution orientations, scrambled at the start. Mirror buttons also support keyboard navigation. This puzzle runs locally.
