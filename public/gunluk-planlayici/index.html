Plan your day

  1. Choose three things you want to finish today. Break large tasks into steps you can complete in one sitting.
  2. Add due dates to time-sensitive tasks. Review overdue and high-priority tasks first.
  3. Check off each finished task. At the end of the day, review what remains.

Where is your data?

Tasks and notes are stored in this browser. No account or internet connection is required.

Clearing browser data can erase your records. Before switching browsers or moving this file, use the Download backup button above to save a copy.

Restoring a backup replaces current tasks and notes with the records in that file. You will be asked to confirm.
