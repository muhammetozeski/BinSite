Weaving Loom

At each crossing, choose warp or weft on top. Those small decisions form the fabric pattern.

Click a top thread header to color a warp thread, or a left header to color a weft thread. Click a crossing to swap the thread on top.

24 warp and 20 weft threads. A dark lift-card square places warp on top. CSV exports the same binary choices. This is a weave draft rather than a fabric stretch or thickness simulation.
