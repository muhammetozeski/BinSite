Navigate in the dark

Move with the arrow keys. Sonar reveals a three-cell radius for the next four moves . The expanding pulse ring marks the revealed area.

Visited passages and walls you bump into stay in memory. Uncollected cores appear only in visible areas. The nearest-signal distance helps guide your search.

Recover all three cores and reach the lower-right exit. Yellow batteries restore one sonar charge. Every attempted move costs 1 oxygen, including blocked moves.

The generated maze is connected. Oxygen equals a computed feasible collection route plus 60 moves for exploration. This is a local single-player game.
